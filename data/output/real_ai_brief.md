## 1. Executive summary

The report describes two Polish unsecured instalment-loan offers, but their representative amounts and terms differ, so they are **not directly comparable customer offers**. Wonga discloses two ways to settle its PLN 2,700 commission: using the customer’s own funds or taking a separate commission loan. The latter removes the upfront payment but adds PLN 768.50 in interest and raises the reported total customer outflow by that amount.

The data is labelled as public lender disclosure, but source extracts are marked **unverified**. APRs are lender-reported and were not independently recalculated. The report does not identify the data as synthetic; its fictional status therefore cannot be established from the supplied information.

## 2. Published lending products and positioning

Provident’s *Pożyczka Samoobsługowa naCoDzień* example is PLN 9,300 over 24 months, with a fixed nominal rate of 7.0% and reported APR of 29.0%. Its reported total credit cost is PLN 2,694.86 and total repayable is PLN 11,994.86. The example includes a PLN 1,887.88 origination fee and PLN 40 preparation fee.

Wonga’s *Pożyczka na raty* example is for new customers: PLN 6,000 over 42 months, with a variable nominal rate of 14.5% and reported APR of 73.69%. Reported total credit cost is PLN 4,407.83 and total repayable is PLN 10,407.83, including a PLN 2,700 commission. The examples differ in amount, term, rate type and customer scope; their headline costs should not be used to rank offers.

## 3. Customer fee-payment options and cash-flow implications

For Wonga’s **upfront-payment option**, the customer pays PLN 2,700 from their own funds before disbursement. The supplied total customer outflow is PLN 10,407.83, with no additional financing cost for the commission. This avoids borrowing to cover the fee, but requires substantial immediate liquidity.

For the **separate commission-loan option**, the disclosed upfront payment is PLN 0. The separate loan is for PLN 2,700, with reported interest of PLN 768.50 and total repayable of PLN 3,468.50. The supplied total customer outflow is PLN 11,176.33—PLN 768.50 above the upfront option. This reduces the initial cash requirement but increases repayment obligations and entails a separate credit agreement and repayment schedule.

These are reported aggregate outflows; they should not be added to the reported total repayable a second time. The separate loan’s APR is reported as 15.5%; it is not an APR for the combined borrowing.

## 4. Financial validation findings

The report marks known cost components and total repayable as matching for both products. Provident’s instalments reconcile. Wonga’s PLN 2,700 instalment difference is marked **EXPLAINED_DIFFERENCE**, consistent with the disclosed commission being excluded from the main loan instalments and settled separately or through another loan. This is an arithmetic reconciliation supported by the recorded fee arrangement—not independent source verification or evidence of regulatory compliance. APRs were not recalculated.

## 5. Evidence limitations and regulatory questions

Both source extracts are unverified, promotions are unknown, and monthly administration fees are unknown. Wonga’s validation also lists preparation and flexible-plan fees as unknown components. Clarify these items, fee treatment, eligibility and applicable terms before comparing or presenting offers. For Wonga’s separate commission loan, obtain legal review of the agreement structure and applicable disclosure, APR and affordability-treatment requirements; the report does not establish non-compliance.

## 6. Product strategy opportunities (hypotheses)

Test whether offering a clearly explained choice between upfront and financed fees addresses different customer liquidity needs. The potential opportunity is to serve customers unable or unwilling to pay a large fee immediately; the trade-off is higher total outflow and added operational complexity. Measure option take-up, completion, customer understanding, complaints, repayment performance and unit economics before drawing conclusions about conversion, profitability or risk.

## 7. Recommended next research steps

Verify current lender disclosures and complete fee schedules; confirm promotions, eligibility and variable-rate conditions; obtain like-for-like examples at common amounts and terms; and validate payment schedules and outflows against agreement documents. Commission legal review of the separate-loan structure and test customer comprehension and outcomes using observed data.
